Ancillary Justice

Ann Leckie, scifi, 03 Aug 2026
I highly recommend this one. This was a reread; it held up very well, though I’m a known sucker for a living weapon. Excellent science fiction that actually knows when it’s saying some Fascist Bullshit, and, as far as I read it, is doing it critically.

Ancillary Sword

Ann Leckie, scifi, 04 Aug 2026\

“Would you ever have stopped loving Lieutenant Awn?”
“If […] she had ever become someone other than who she was.”

I highly recommend this book. It’s a good second installment; it pulses with the same anger as Ancillary Justice. Perhaps more diluted, but nevertheless.

Ancillary Mercy

Ann Leckie, scifi, 04 Aug 2026
I think it’s still worth reading, but I would not recommend this book on its own merit. It feels like it loses the anger, feels like it’s wrapped up in its conclusion. Leckie did not stick the landing. Sword and Justice were telegraphed by what they were, Mercy was telegraphed for what it was. Does that make sense? It’s not my favourite. It’s still worth reading, but if it was a single book without the context of the other two, I would not be recommending it.
