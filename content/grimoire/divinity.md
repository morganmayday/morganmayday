> Mon chien est athée: il ne crois plus en moi.
>
> François Cavanna

## i: suffering.

there is a lot of suffering in this world.

i don't think i need to pull a number or a figure or quotes for this claim. you can look outside. even if capitalism and carnism and hierarchy are not *evils* in your evaluation, they, objectively, cause a lot of suffering as they are in the present day; sweatshops and factory farms and genocide, objectively, *cause suffering.*

i, necessarily, do not believe in God. if i believed in God, well. you know what i think about towers.

## ii: towers.

the saying "no gods, no masters" is bandied about a lot in leftist circles, often by people who either have a God or at least partly embrace hierarchy or both.

i do not entirely understand this. why, i ask, do you say "no gods" when you *have* a God? why, i ask, do you say "no masters" when you readily embrace hierarchical structures with no attempts to mitigate their effects? i find an inherent contradiction here that i cannot create a superposition within. (or---maybe, i can, but we'll get to that.) you build a tower whilst declaiming them. 

is this lying to me, i want to ask, or is it lying to yourself?

i do not ask it. it would do me no good. there are sharper questions i could ask.

## III: divinity.

of course, there is, to me, divinity. it is not some outside force, nor is it some deity on high or low. it is the thing that is all things. it is the act of creation. it is the connections between us all, cooperation and community and harmony. it is the grandeur of the universe and every little thing within it. it is, put simply, a blooming. this is why the alchemy of self you engage in when you choose what you want to become and act on it is holy: it is creation, of *Yourself.*

this is, to those who seek to destroy and consume and multiply and conquer, anathema.

I am something divine. so is everyone else. I make of myself a vessel so that I can better act in its interests. there are others who seek to cut that part of themselves out, to kill and to consume without an ounce of themself that will act against it. I pick up a weapon in the shape of a woman that I might collaborate better, that I might *act* better, that I might *create* better, that I might begin to more than dream of destroying what destroys me.

## iv: duty.

if there is a God that presides over all this suffering, it is my duty to kill Him, not because i am exceptional or strange or something *rare*, but because I dream of something *better* than this miserable death spiral we find the world in.

I'll tell you a secret, dear reader: *anyone* can do that.
